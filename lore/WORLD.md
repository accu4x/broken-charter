# The world

Canon for Broken Charter. Game text must agree with this file. Agreed with Dan in the first
brainstorm (2026-10-08); changes to canon are his.

## The fall

The empire held its lands together with an ancient **ward**. Its own court archmage, chasing
immortality, broke it, and became the **Necromancer**. With the ward gone the empire collapsed.

The last emperor's final act was to sign the **charters**: writs that gave the adventurers'
guilds legal authority to defend what remained. The throne has stood empty since. Every lord
wants the guilds on their side, and the guilds are what is left holding the realm together.

## Why every road is new

Beyond the town walls the land is unstable. Without the ward the **wilds shift**: paths move,
ruins surface and sink, and no map lasts a season. Every expedition is a fresh road.

## The returning darkness

The Necromancer's power waxes and wanes in cycles. Each rising is a season, and each time the
dead march harder. What the guild builds between risings is what survives them.

## The threats

| Faction | What they are | How they fight | What beats them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The dead** | The Necromancer's army: skeletons, ghouls, wraiths, bone knights. The main enemy. | Relentless. Poison and bleeding do nothing to them. | Holy power. |
| **Goblins** | Opportunists pouring through the broken frontier. Not allied with the dead. | Swarms of weak fighters, slingers and shamans. | Blows that hit them all at once. |
| **Trolls** | Frontier brutes that come with the goblins. | Heavy blows; they knit their wounds back. | Fire, which stops the healing. |
| **Lizardkin** | The people of the marshes the empire drained. They are taking the land back. | Ambush from the water; venom. | Scouts who see them coming; priests who cleanse the venom. |

The lizardkin are the one people the guild might one day bargain with rather than fight.

## The regions

| Region | Contract | Threat | What it yields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Sunken Crypt** | The dead walk the old imperial vaults; a town priest pays to put them back. | The dead, led by a Bone Knight. | Bone dust, grave iron. |
| **The Thornwood** | Warbands block the forest road; a lord wants it open. | Goblins and trolls, led by Old Mossback. | Goblin scrap, troll hide. |
| **The Mirefen** | Settlers drained the marsh; the lizardkin want it back. The settlers are paying, for now. | Lizardkin, led by the Fen Matriarch. | Lizard scale, venom sacs. |
| **The imperial capital** | Not yet open. The Necromancer's heartland, and the last region. | The dead. | Unknown. |

## The guild

Your guild holds one of the charters. It takes contracts, recruits from whoever walks in, trains
them, arms them from what its contracts bring home, and buries the ones it loses. The names of
the fallen are kept on a wall in the hall.
